Prerequisite

2021 — 2025

Prerequisite is the first painting project he created after arriving in Poland. Before then, his artistic practice was rooted in drawing, working primarily with pencil, charcoal, graphite, and pastel. The project brings together a range of themes, drawn from personal experiences, the stories of others, personal reflections and messages he wanted to share. Despite their differences, each work is connected by a common purpose: growth. Every painting represents a lesson, a challenge, and a step toward transforming an artistic vision into a tangible practice. Prerequisite remains deeply personal because it marks the beginning of his journey as a painter. The foundation from which Narrative Illusionism would later emerge.
