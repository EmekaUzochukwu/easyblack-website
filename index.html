Narrative Illusionism

Narrative Illusionism is a painting approach that constructs portraits through fragmented imagery, allowing individual visual elements to unite into a complete identity while revealing the stories and experiences that shape the subject.

Working primarily in oil on canvas, Easyblack explores identity through layered compositions inspired by real-life events and the lived experiences of people. Fragmentation becomes a reflection of the complexity of human existence — the memories, emotions, and circumstances that remain unseen beneath the surface.

About Easyblack

Easyblack is a Nigerian-born, Poland-based self-taught painter working primarily in oil on canvas. His practice explores identity through Narrative Illusionism, creating portraits from fragmented imagery that reveal the stories and experiences shaping each individual.
