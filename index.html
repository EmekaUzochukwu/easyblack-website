Narrative Illusionism

Narrative Illusionism constructs portraits through fragmented imagery, allowing separate visual elements to unite into a complete identity while revealing the stories and experiences that shape each subject.

Working primarily in oil on canvas, Easyblack draws inspiration from real-life events and lived experiences. Fragmentation becomes a metaphor for memory, identity, resilience and human complexity, inviting viewers to slow down and discover what lies beneath the surface.

Biography

Easyblack is a Nigerian-born, Poland-based self-taught painter working primarily in oil on canvas. His work reconstructs identity through Narrative Illusionism, creating portraits built from fragmented imagery that reveal the experiences, emotions, and memories shaping each individual.
