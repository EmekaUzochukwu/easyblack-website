Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Your privacy matters. This policy explains how personal information is handled when you visit easyblackoffice.com, subscribe to The EASYBLACK Letter or contact me through this website.

Who I am

This website is operated by Emeka Uzochukwu, an individual artist working under the name EASYBLACK.

For privacy-related questions, you can contact me at: info@easyblackoffice.com .

Information I collect

Depending on how you use the website, I may receive:

  • Your email address when you subscribe to the newsletter.
  • Your name, email address and message when you use the contact form.
  • The title of an artwork associated with your enquiry, where applicable.
  • Technical information processed by the website and its service providers, such as IP address and browser information.

How your information is used

Your information is used to manage newsletter subscriptions, respond to messages and artwork enquiries, communicate about potential purchases or commissions, and help maintain the security and operation of the website.

Newsletter subscriptions are based on your consent. Contact enquiries are handled to respond to your request and, where relevant, take steps towards a potential agreement. Website security may involve processing based on legitimate interests.

Newsletter and contact services

The EASYBLACK Letter is managed through Brevo. The signup process uses double opt-in: after entering your email address, you must confirm your subscription through an email link before becoming a confirmed subscriber. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link in a newsletter.

The newsletter form uses Google reCAPTCHA for spam protection.

Messages submitted through the contact form are processed using Formspree so that I can receive and respond to your enquiries.

Website services and cookies

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages and uses Google Fonts to display its typography. These services may process technical information when your browser connects to them.

The website does not currently use a dedicated analytics platform. Third-party services used by the website may nevertheless use cookies or similar technologies for functionality and security.

How long information is kept

Information is kept only for as long as necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, or where retention is required by law. Newsletter information is kept while a subscription remains active; contact messages are kept as long as needed to handle the enquiry and any necessary follow-up.

Your privacy rights

Under applicable data protection law, including the GDPR, you may have the right to access, correct or request deletion of your personal information, restrict or object to certain processing, request data portability where applicable, and withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

To make a request, email info@easyblackoffice.com .

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the President of the Polish Personal Data Protection Office (UODO).

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the website or its services change. The latest version will be published on this page.

Contact

For questions about this policy or the handling of your personal information, contact:
